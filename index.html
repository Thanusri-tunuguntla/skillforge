FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers. If you don't find what you need here, reach out to us directly.

Are the courses completely free?
▾
We offer a mix of free and premium content. All our learning resources, cheat sheets, and introductory modules are completely free. Full courses with mentorship, mock interviews, and certificates are available through our affordable premium plans starting at ₹499/month.
Do I need prior coding experience to start?
▾
Not at all! Our Python Programming and Data Science courses are designed for absolute beginners. We start from the basics and gradually build up your skills. All you need is curiosity and dedication.
How do the internship alerts work?
▾
Once you sign up for internship alerts, we curate opportunities from top companies every week and send them directly to your email. You can set preferences by domain (AI, web dev, data science) and location. We also provide tips on how to apply effectively for each opportunity.
Can I get a certificate after completing a course?
▾
Yes! All premium course completions come with an industry-recognized SkillForge certificate that you can share on LinkedIn and add to your resume. Our certificates are verified digitally and respected by hiring managers at top tech companies.
How are mock interviews conducted?
▾
Mock interviews are 45-minute sessions conducted via video call with our mentors who are working professionals at top companies. They simulate real interview conditions, ask company-specific questions, and provide detailed written feedback within 24 hours. Book a session through our contact page.
What makes SkillForge different from other platforms?
▾
Unlike generic MOOCs, SkillForge is built specifically for Indian students entering the tech job market. We combine structured learning, personalized mentorship, real internship connections, and career coaching in one platform. Our content is constantly updated with the latest industry trends including Generative AI.
Is there community support available?
▾
Absolutely! Join our Discord community of 5000+ students where you can ask questions, share projects, find study partners, and get peer support. We also host weekly live Q&A sessions and webinars with industry experts — all free for enrolled students.
